02 — the cleanup

Spoken mess in.
Written sentence out.

People stutter, correct themselves, and say filler words. Quotal's deterministic engine and local neural polish rewrite mid-sentence course-corrections in real time.

you said“umm so aaj milte hai, okay uh?”

you get“So aaj milte hai, okay?”

you said“let’s meet at five, no wait, six pm”

you get“Let’s meet at 6 PM.”

you said“send it over, badi, plz”

you get“Send it over, buddy, please.”

you said“press control shift p to open command palette”

you get“Press Ctrl+Shift+P to open command palette.”

Deterministic: filler sounds (um uh hmm ah er…) stripped, no wait / sorry / I mean / no no resolves to the final correction, spoken programming shortcuts expanded (package dot json → package.json, control shift p → Ctrl+Shift+P). Careful exceptions: hum (“we” in Hindi) and err are never stripped.

you said“basically you know the meeting was like three things first intro then demo and follow up email”

you get“The meeting covered three things: 1) intro, 2) demo, 3) follow-up email.”

you said“i didnt sent the dont file yesterday”

you get“I didn’t send the file yesterday.”

A small local language model (Qwen 2.5 0.5B, ~390 MB) reflows grammar, lists, and prose automatically. Download it on demand with 1-click in the dashboard; runs on CPU, faster on CUDA. Still zero cloud.

whisper heard“uh lets meet at five um no wait six”

you get“uh lets meet at five um no wait six”

Byte-for-byte Whisper output. Capitalisation and punctuation untouched. Ideal for transcripts, subtitles, or verbatim dictation.

05 — asked often

Fair questions.

01Does my voice ever leave my computer?

No. Never. The microphone buffer is streamed directly to a Whisper model stored on your local disk. Once transcribed, deterministic rules and local neural models format the text. You can completely disconnect your internet, and Quotal continues to work with zero disruption.

02How do Minor & Major OTA Updates work?

Traditional apps force you to re-download 500+ MB installers for every minor fix. Quotal uses a dual-engine OTA system: when we ship new UI enhancements, languages, or smart cleaner rules, Quotal auto-detects that it's a minor update, downloads it quickly in the background, extracts it, and restarts in 1 second.

03How does the smart self-correction work?

If you say “Let's meet at 5, no wait, 6 PM”, Quotal recognizes the spoken correction token (no wait, actually, sorry, no no) and automatically replaces the retracted number/time with your intended choice, resulting in “Let's meet at 6 PM.”

04Can I change the Right Alt or F8 hotkey?

Yes. You can switch between Right Alt and F8 directly in the dashboard Settings. For custom keycodes (like Caps Lock or extra mouse buttons), you can edit the Win32 keycodes in settings.json.

05How fast is Quotal on CPU without a dedicated GPU?

With our physical core thread affinity and CTranslate2 greedy decoding optimizations, Whisper Base transcribes 12 seconds of speech in just 2.0 seconds on standard Intel/AMD laptop CPUs (6x faster than real-time).

06Is Quotal free and open-source?

Yes! Quotal is 100% free forever under the permissive MIT license. You can inspect the code, modify the rules, and contribute on GitHub.
